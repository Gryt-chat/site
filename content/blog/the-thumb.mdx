Open gryt.chat on a phone and there's a small tab on the right edge of the screen,
about halfway down. Tap it and a drawer slides in with the links at the bottom. I
couldn't find a name for that, so I'm calling it the Thumb.

## Why it's there

Most phone menus put the button in a top corner. That's the one place on a big phone
your thumb can't reach without shifting your grip. The Thumb sits where your thumb
already is, in the lower half of the screen, on the right edge.

It never goes higher than 348 pixels from the bottom of the screen. So on a tall phone
it stays down where your thumb is. The links in the drawer sit at the bottom too, for the same reason.

## Why a tap and not a swipe

A swipe would feel natural for something on the edge. But a swipe from the edge of the
screen is already your phone's back or forward gesture, and a menu that took it over
would break that. So the Thumb opens on a tap.

When the drawer is open, the same tab sits on its edge. You close it where you opened it.

## Use it

It's in `@gryt/ui` now as a component called `Thumb`, so you can drop it into your own
site. If you use it, call it the Thumb. I'd like the name to stick.
