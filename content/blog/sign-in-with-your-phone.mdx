Setting Gryt up on a new computer used to mean signing in, joining your servers again and
starting with empty DMs. If Gryt is already on your phone, that's one scan now.

## Scan the code

Open Settings → Account on the computer. If you're not signed in, there's a code under
**Sign in with your phone**.

<Image src="/blog/sign-in-with-your-phone/settings-account.webp" alt="Gryt's Settings, Account page, not signed in, with a Sign in with Gryt button and below it a QR code next to the text 'Sign in with your phone. Point your phone's camera at the code. Gryt opens there, and your account, servers and messages come over to this computer.' and a short code to type instead" width="1600" height="1167" />

Point your phone's camera at it and Gryt opens on the phone. You can also scan it from
**Scan a code** on the You tab. Both devices then show a few small pictures. Check they're
the same on both, approve on the phone, and the computer gets your account, every server
you're in and your old messages. You don't have to join a server first. If scanning
doesn't work, the computer also shows a short code you can type into **Link a device** on
the phone.

It works the other way round too. Settings → Your devices has **Link a device** for adding
another one, and **Link this device** for setting up the one you're on. The phone has
both in its settings too.

<Image src="/blog/sign-in-with-your-phone/phone-link.webp" alt="Two phone screens. Left, Link this device shows a QR code and the code 1M1Q-Z9TX to type on a device you already use. Right, after linking, a green tick and 'Linked from Chrome on macOS. Your message history goes back to 10 November 2026.' above a Done button" width="1400" height="1056" />

While it links, both sides list each step from Approve to Done. Keep Gryt open on both
until it says Done. The screen stays on until then, so the phone won't lock halfway.

## Where your old messages go

The link itself goes through Gryt's linking service: your keys, your account and the list
of your servers. It's sealed so the service can't read it, and it's small. The pictures
aren't sent anywhere. Each device works them out on its own, which is why matching
pictures mean nobody got in between.

Your old messages don't go through it. Since 1.15.7 they wait for the new device on one
of your own servers, locked so that server can't read them either. The new device fetches
them, and the server deletes them as they're picked up, or after an hour at the latest.

Whoever runs a server can turn this off in the server's settings, under **Linking a new
device**. If none of your servers will carry your messages, both devices tell you that
they stay on the one you linked from, and the rest of the link still goes through.

1.15.8 fixed one thing here. Linking a phone from a computer, or a computer from a phone,
said your messages came across, and then they didn't show in your chats. They do now.
Between two computers it always worked.

## Getting the phone app

The phone app is still in testing, so it isn't in the App Store or on Google Play for
everyone yet. Anyone can join the test.

**iPhone**

1. Open the [TestFlight invite](https://testflight.apple.com/join/6sckEEFx) on your
   iPhone.
2. If you don't have TestFlight, it asks you to install it from the App Store first. Then
   open the invite again.
3. Tap Install. New builds show up in TestFlight.

**Android**

1. Join the [gryt-testers group](https://groups.google.com/g/gryt-testers) with the Google
   account you use on your phone. Anyone can join.
2. [Opt in to the test](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/chat.gryt.mobile) with the
   same account.
3. Install [Gryt from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=chat.gryt.mobile).

The same steps are on the [download page](/download#phone-test). Then sign in on the
phone, or start fresh and join a server, and you're ready to scan.

Android testers help the most right now. Google wants twelve people on the test for
fourteen days in a row before Gryt can go on Google Play for everyone.

If something breaks, tell me on [our Gryt server](https://gryt.chat/community) or with
Give feedback in the app's settings.
