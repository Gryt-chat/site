Direct messages in Gryt worked, but they didn't feel like anything. The list was a
list of names, the chat looked like a channel, and nothing told you who the person
on the other end was. 1.14 changes all three, on the desktop and on the phone.

![The Messages space on the desktop. On the left a list of conversations with Al and Carlo pinned at the top, then a group called Saturday raid and a few more. In the middle the chat with Al. On the right Al's member card, green, showing he's playing Counter-Strike 2, with pinned messages and shared files underneath](/blog/dm-desktop.webp)

## What's in it

Messages has its own place now, above your servers on the desktop and as a tab on
the phone. Every conversation from every server you're on is in one list, newest
first. Unread ones are bold with a count.

You can pin the people you talk to most. They stay on top in the order you drag
them into, and a new message doesn't shuffle them around. Pins live on your device
only, so the server never learns who you pinned. The cost is that they don't follow
you to another device yet.

The server name only shows up when it has to. If you know two people called Ole on
two servers, you get two rows, and each says which server it's from. Everyone else
is just a name.

The panel on the right is the person's own member card, with the colour and banner
they picked, what they're playing, and what's pinned and shared in the conversation
below it. It can be hidden. Groups show their members there instead, and anybody in
a group can rename it or add people.

The previews under each name come from this device's history. The server only
stores encrypted messages, so it has nothing it could send you to show there, and
the bottom of the list says so.

![Three phone screens. The Messages tab with the same pinned list. The chat with Al. And Al's card opened as a sheet from the bottom, after tapping his name](/blog/dm-phone.webp)

The phone got the same thing: a Messages tab between Servers and Search, the same
pinned list, and the card as a sheet when you tap someone's name.

## Drawn first, built second

Every screen here was drawn before any of it was built, on a canvas in Claude
Design. Claude drew the boards and I said what was wrong with them, again and again.
It's the first time I've done Gryt that way, and it was a lot cheaper to throw a
board away than a pull request.

Plenty got thrown away. The list started out split into Unread and Recent, until it
was obvious that a conversation jumps sections the moment you read it. The right
panel was the whole card at full height for a while, which looked great and left no
room for anything else. One draft put a call bar at the bottom left of the screen,
and I recognised it as Discord's before I'd finished looking at it.

Calls were the hardest part. Gryt already had controls for a call at the bottom of
the server rail, and they've been there since about 2021. They work, but you have
to know they're there. On the desktop they grew into a green capsule with the
call's face on top, so you can see who's talking and get back to the call from
anywhere.

The phone went through three versions. A bar across the top pushed everything down.
A slot in the tab bar brought back a problem we'd already removed once. So it's the
round button that floats above the tabs, the same one as before, but it shows the
call's face now and lights up when someone talks. Holding it gives you mute, deafen
and leave without opening the call.

![Two phone screens. The Messages tab with a round green button floating above the tab bar, showing the faces of the group in the call. Next to it the call itself, four people in tiles with controls along the bottom](/blog/dm-phone-call.webp)

Groups used to get an egg as a picture when nobody had uploaded one. It looked like
Easter. Now they get up to four of their members in a rounded square, which changes
as people join and leave.

## When a conversation isn't encrypted

Direct messages are encrypted on your device. That only works when everyone in the
conversation has a key the app will accept, and sometimes somebody doesn't. Usually
their app is too old.

Gryt used to say so in one line above the messages. Now each message that went out unencrypted gets a small red dot, the box you type in says
"(not encrypted)", and the header says Not encrypted. Click that and it tells you
why, and what you can do about it.

![The chat with Mira, marked Not encrypted. A box under the header explains that whoever runs LAN Lounge can read what's sent, because Mira's app hasn't set up a key for this server yet. It says there's nothing to do on your side, offers a button to ask Mira to update, and has a button to stop marking messages here](/blog/dm-not-encrypted.webp)

Most of the time there's nothing to do on your side. Their app sets up a key by
itself the next time they open an up-to-date Gryt, the conversation switches to
encrypted on its own, and a line in the chat marks where that happened. If you'd
rather they got on with it, Ask Mira to update sends one short message saying why.

If you've seen the dots and don't care, Stop marking messages here takes them away
for that conversation. The header keeps saying it.

## Who added whom

When someone adds a person to a group, everyone in it now sees a line saying who
added whom. Nobody turns up in a group without the others knowing.

![The Saturday raid group with a line in the chat reading "Al added Sivert", and under it that Sivert only sees messages from here on, and what was said before stays encrypted for the people who were there](/blog/dm-group-added.webp)

The line also says what the new person can read. In an encrypted group that's
messages from that point on. What was said before stays with the people who were
there. If the group isn't encrypted, the line says that the new person can read
the history too, because right now they can. Cutting that off at the moment they
joined needs a change on the server, and that's next.

## A server that can't be reached

This one came out of testing the rest. When a server stopped answering, the top
of the screen fell apart. Now the header stays and says Can't reach it in yellow,
and the middle of the screen says the server might be down, or your network can't
see it. Try now tries straight away, and Check the connection runs through what
might be wrong.

![A server called LAN Lounge marked Can't reach it, with a message in the middle saying it isn't answering, and buttons to try now or check the connection](/blog/dm-unreachable.webp)

## Getting it

This is in 1.14. The desktop app updates itself, and the phone app follows in
the next test builds. The parts that need the server,
like the "added" line, need your server on 1.12 or newer.
